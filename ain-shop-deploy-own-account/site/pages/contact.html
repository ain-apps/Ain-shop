Contact and help

AIN Shop is made by an individual developer in Oak Park, Michigan, USA. We are happy to help.

Mailing address: 21700 Greenfield Rd, Ste 121 Unit #2118, Oak Park, MI 48237, USA

Email us: ainshop.corrections@mail.tab.bot

How to get help

  • Write to ainshop.corrections@mail.tab.bot. Plain words are fine — you can dictate the email by voice.
  • Tell us what you tried, what happened, and your phone type (for example iPhone).
  • We usually answer within 2 business days.

Found a mistake?

If AIN Shop said or showed something wrong (a price, a product, a word), please tell us. Email corrections to ainshop.corrections@mail.tab.bot. In the app you can also say “report a mistake”.

Email a correction

Questions about an order

AIN Shop does not sell or ship products. For an order, delivery, refund or warranty, please contact the store you bought from.

Delete your data

AIN Shop has no accounts, and it does not keep your voice. To ask us to delete anything we hold that can be linked to you, email ainshop.corrections@mail.tab.bot with the subject “Delete my data”. We will delete it within 30 days and tell you when it is done. To delete what is saved on your phone (language, shopping list, “Buy again” history, ZIP code, settings), use this button, or clear website data for ainshop.sites.tab.bot in your phone’s settings.
